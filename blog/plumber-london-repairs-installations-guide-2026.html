Plumber in London: common repairs, installations and how to get a clear quote.

Published 10 September 2026 · 6 min read · By Nikita Denysiuk, Hausio

Hausio provides plumbing repairs and installations across Greater London for common water, waste and bathroom jobs. This includes leaking taps, sink wastes, toilets, appliance connections, radiator changes and bathroom fittings. The price is confirmed for the specific job after the fault, access and likely parts have been reviewed.

The quickest route to a useful plumbing quote is a clear description supported by photos or a short video. This guide explains what to send, which jobs can often be priced remotely and when an inspection is the sensible next step.

Common plumbing jobs in London homes

  • Leaks and drips: visible leaks from taps, exposed pipework, traps, wastes and accessible joints.
  • Taps and sinks: tap replacement, cartridges, basin and sink wastes, traps and resealing around fittings.
  • Toilets: flush mechanisms, fill valves, seats, seals and selected toilet replacements.
  • Appliance connections: washing machines and dishwashers where suitable water, waste and electrical connections already exist.
  • Radiators: valve changes, replacement radiators and related water-side work after the system and access are assessed.
  • Bathroom finishing: fitting selected sanitary items, shower accessories, waste connections and silicone finishing.

What to do when you find a leak

If water is escaping quickly, turn off the relevant isolation valve or the property's main stopcock if it is safe and accessible. Move belongings away from the water and avoid electrical fittings or sockets in the affected area. Then send Hausio a photograph or video showing both the leak and the wider installation.

A stain on a ceiling or wall does not always show where the water began. The source can be above or some distance away, so hidden leaks may need investigation before repair work can be quoted accurately.

Can a plumber quote from a photo?

Visible, defined jobs can often be priced from good photographs. A clear image of the complete tap, trap, toilet cistern or appliance connection helps identify the likely part and the space available to work. Include the make or model where it is visible.

An inspection is more likely when pipework is concealed, the source of a leak is uncertain, access is restricted, several fittings are affected or the proposed installation requires pipe rerouting. The purpose of the visit is to define the repair rather than guess at it.

How Hausio prepares a plumbing quote

The quote considers labour, likely parts, access and the possibility of further investigation. Materials are listed separately unless the written scope says they are included. If the opened fitting reveals a different fault, the plumber explains the finding and agrees any change before additional work continues.

For the first enquiry, send your postcode, the type of fitting, when the problem started, whether water can be isolated and photos from both close and wider angles. Mention any parking or building-access restrictions.

Plumbing, boilers and gas work

Water plumbing and gas work are different scopes. Work on gas appliances, gas pipework and boilers must be undertaken by a suitably qualified Gas Safe registered engineer. Hausio does not describe a general plumber as qualified for gas work. If the enquiry involves a boiler or gas appliance, say so immediately so the correct route can be considered.

Repairs versus planned installations

A repair starts with the fault: what leaks, blocks, fails to fill or no longer operates. A planned installation starts with compatibility: dimensions, connections, water pressure, drainage, wall construction and the item being fitted. Product links and specification sheets help prevent a new tap, sink or radiator arriving without the correct connections or space.
