Building Works FAQ

Do you give an hourly building rate?

Building projects vary too much for one honest hourly headline. We price the agreed project after reviewing the scope, materials, access and finish.

Can Hausio carry out foundation work?

Yes, subject to inspection and the correct specification. Structural foundation work must follow the engineer's design and applicable Building Control requirements before work begins.

Do you supply tiles, wallpaper and glass?

We can price supply or work with customer-supplied finishes. The quote states quantities, allowances and who purchases each item.

Is glazing certification included?

The quote identifies any safety-glass, Building Regulations or competent-person requirements. Work requiring certification is assigned only through an appropriately qualified route.
