## Spend rule

Use the free map and directory profiles first. From the £500 budget, reserve at least £350 for one measured lead-source trial rather than splitting it across several marketplaces. Track calls and booking URLs separately for each platform. Do not renew a paid membership without recorded enquiries, booked jobs, revenue and gross profit.
