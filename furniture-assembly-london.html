How a London assembly visit works

  1. 01

    Tell us what's in the box

    Model name (Pax, Malm, etc.) or a photo of the IKEA receipt is enough. For Wayfair-type pieces, the product link tells us what we're dealing with.

  2. 02

    Pick a slot — or combine with pickup

    If IKEA Wembley delivery is £40 and you'd rather we collect-and-build, we'll quote a combined man-and-van + assembly slot. Often cheaper than store delivery.

  3. 03

    We build, you live around us

    One Pax in a bedroom blocks the room for ~2 hours. We'll tell you on arrival roughly when we'll be done so you can plan around it. Cardboard goes out with us if you'd like.

Furniture assembly FAQ

How much for Pax assembly in London?

A standard Pax frame with two doors is £85 flat-rate. Add £15 per extra pair of doors and £20 for glide-rail drawer kits. Typically 1.5–2 hours' work; tipped into place not lifted, so no cracked side panels in low-ceiling flats.

Do you bring tools?

Yes — drill, hex keys, mallet, spirit level, stud finder, tape measure. You only need to hold on to the IKEA fittings bag (cams, dowels, screws) — those are model-specific.

What if parts are missing?

We check the manifest before opening the sealed fittings bag. If anything is genuinely missing, we stop at the latest safe stage, log the IKEA part number, and return for a short follow-up at £45 once you've reordered the spare — no second minimum.
