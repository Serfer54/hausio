Garden Maintenance FAQ

Is garden maintenance the same as garden clearance?

No. Maintenance covers mowing, trimming, pruning and weeding. Large volumes, sheds and unwanted items are priced through our separate garden-clearance service.

Do you offer regular visits?

Yes. Frequency depends on the garden and season; we can quote fortnightly or monthly care after seeing the space.

Do you remove green waste?

Bagging and removal can be included. Send photos so the likely volume and disposal cost can be shown separately.

Do you carry out major tree work?

Large trees, protected trees and work needing specialist climbing or permissions require an appropriately qualified tree professional and are quoted separately.
