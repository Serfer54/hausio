How it works

Booked in 60 seconds. Paid for only when it's done.

Getting something done at home shouldn't be a project in itself. Here's exactly how Hausio works — from the tap that books it to the moment your home is sorted — with no deposit and no surprises along the way.

The process

Four steps, start to finish

01

Choose your service & book

Pick what you need — a clean, a move, a handyman or a specialist job — pop in your postcode and a date, and you're booked. The whole thing takes about a minute online, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it.

  • Online in ~60 seconds
  • Or WhatsApp
  • 7 days a week
  • 8am–8pm
02

See your price up front

You get a clear, fixed price before you commit — no call-out fees, no hourly surprises, no small print. And there's £0 to pay at this point: booking costs you nothing.

  • Fixed pricing
  • No call-out fee
  • £0 deposit
03

A vetted pro shows up

On the day, a DBS-checked, fully insured Hausio professional arrives on time with the right tools. They know the local parking, access and building quirks — so the job just gets done, not debated on the doorstep.

  • DBS-checked
  • £2M insured
  • On time
  • Right tools
04

Pay only when it's done

You settle up after the work is finished and you're happy with it — by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. No cash, no upfront deposit, no risk. If something isn't right, we put it right.

  • Pay after the job
  • Card · Apple Pay · Google Pay
  • No cash needed

Booking questions

How quickly can you come?

Book before 10am and we can usually offer a same-day slot; otherwise next-day and same-week are standard across London. A small same-day surcharge applies.

Do I really pay nothing upfront?

Yes. There's £0 to pay at booking — you settle only once the job is finished and you're satisfied with the work.

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay after the visit. No cash required and no deposit.

Can I reschedule?

Yes — just let us know ahead of time and we'll move your slot, no fuss.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell us and we'll put it right. Every job is done by a DBS-checked, fully insured professional, and we stand behind it.
