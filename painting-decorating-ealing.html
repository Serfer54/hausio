Painting & Decorating in Ealing

Painting and decorating in Ealing is mostly single rooms and whole-flat repaints in period conversions and lettings. Prep is most of the job — sanding, filling and taping done properly — with Dulux Trade and Farrow & Ball finishes cut in by brush.

Single rooms

Walls and ceiling with proper prep — sanding, filling holes, taping skirting and architraves, dust-sheeting the floor. Two coats minimum, three if the base colour bleeds through (we tell you up front).

  • £320 standard room
  • Prep + 2 coats included
  • Dulux Trade or F&B
  • Three coats if base bleeds

Whole flat

Studio to 3-bed flats end-to-end, typically 3–6 painter-days. We sequence rooms so you keep one habitable while the rest dries, and we stage furniture rather than move it out.

  • Studio: 2 painter-days
  • 2-bed: 4–5 painter-days
  • Sequenced rooms (1 habitable)
  • Furniture staged not moved

Period property & external

Sash window restoration (rebrush after sand-back, glaze putty), external doors (primer + 2 topcoats), Farrow & Ball matte finishes done right (cutting in by brush, not roller). Lincrusta and picture rail work on quote.

  • Sash window per side £85
  • External door £140
  • F&B matte specialist
  • Lincrusta & picture rail on quote
