A CLEAR WAY TO WORK TOGETHER

Partnership essentials

  • 10% service fee on labour. Materials and other expenses require separate approval. Any applicable tax treatment and the exact payout are clarified before you accept work.
  • Payments through Hausio. Customers pay Hausio for Hausio bookings. Changes to the job or price must be agreed through us.
  • Written terms before you start. We agree payout timing, cancellations, complaints and any rework before your first job.
  • Reliable, suitable partners. Be ready to provide references and discuss your experience, equipment and relevant insurance or qualifications.
  • Flexible opportunities. Applying does not guarantee acceptance or a minimum number of jobs. Areas are not exclusive.

This is an application to join our network, not a signed partnership agreement.
