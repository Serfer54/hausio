Plumbing FAQ

Do you handle emergency plumbing?

We accept urgent enquiries during our normal hours, 08:00–20:00. Availability and the price are confirmed before attendance.

Can you repair boilers or gas appliances?

Gas and boiler work is only undertaken by a Gas Safe registered engineer. We do not present general plumbing staff as qualified for gas work.

Can you quote from a photo?

Often yes for taps, traps, toilet parts and appliance connections. Hidden leaks or pipe rerouting may need an inspection.

Are parts included?

Parts and materials are listed separately in the quote unless the written scope says they are included.
