How a London TV mount visit works

  1. 01

    Send us the TV model & wall photo

    The TV model number tells us the VESA pattern and weight. A photo of the wall (and what's plugged in where) tells us cable run, power socket position, and whether we need a stud detector or a hammer drill.

  2. 02

    We confirm price & bracket

    Fixed by screen size. If you need a bracket, we bring one to match — at the trade price we paid, with the receipt if you want it.

  3. 03

    One visit, sofa-tested

    Mount goes up, TV goes on, we check the angle from the sofa, run the power-on test, and walk you through the bracket's tilt range so you can adjust later.

TV mounting FAQ

How much to mount a 55-inch TV?

£75 fixed in London, whether the wall is plasterboard, brick or reinforced concrete. Add £40 for in-wall cable concealment, £20 for surface trunking on solid masonry.

Can you mount above a fireplace?

Yes, but we'll usually try to talk you out of it. Heat above an active fire voids most TV warranties, and the viewing angle is bad for your neck. On a sealed electric or decorative-only chimney breast it's fine — a tilt bracket helps. For active fires, a side-of-chimney pull-out arm is the better solution.

Do you supply brackets?

Either way works. Bring your own, or we bring one at trade cost — £25–£60 for fixed/tilt, £80–£140 for full-motion arms. No markup. If you've already got one, send us the model number so we can confirm the VESA and weight rating.
