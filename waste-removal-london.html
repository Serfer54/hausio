Why a licensed waste carrier matters

It's the single biggest difference between a £60 Facebook van and an Environment-Agency-registered firm — and the difference shows up if anything goes wrong.

  • Householder liability stays with you

    Under the Environmental Protection Act 1990, the householder is responsible for ensuring waste is given to a licensed carrier. Unlicensed van fly-tips it? You get the fine.

  • Transfer note in your inbox

    Every Hausio job ends with an emailed waste transfer note showing what was collected, our CBDU number, and the registered transfer station. Keep it 2 years.

  • Landlords & businesses

    If you're a landlord or running a business, you're legally required to have transfer notes on file. We format ours for landlord/agency record-keeping by default.

  • Tip fees, never extras

    Quoted price includes the per-tonne tip charge at the transfer station. We don't sting you with a "disposal fee" at the end of the job once the van's loaded.

Waste removal FAQ

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes — Upper Tier Waste Carrier with the Environment Agency. We issue a Duty of Care waste transfer note (with our CBDU number) by email for every job. Under the Environmental Protection Act 1990 the householder is liable for fly-tipping by unlicensed carriers — keep that transfer note.

How much to clear a 2-bed flat?

A typical 2-bed clearance fits a full Luton: £280, two loaders, drive, tip fees, transfer note included. A full strip-out with loft and kitchen would be around £500 (two Luton loads). We quote from a photo so there are no surprises on the day.

Do you take mattresses?

Yes — £55 per mattress as a single item, or free if it's part of a half or full Luton load. Mattresses go to a hard-to-recycle waste stream that most London councils stopped collecting kerbside — that's our problem, not yours.
